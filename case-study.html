Case study · 2 minutes · works offline in the desktop app

One city, one freight corridor.

Pune, planned as an urban local body with ₹10 crore under PM E-DRIVE; then NH-48 Delhi → Jaipur, planned for heavy vehicles. Every number is computed live in the app from the bundled data.

What it shows

  1. Pune. PM E-DRIVE categories, BEE benchmarks and subsidy inside the optimiser; each station's configuration, cost and peak-hour queue; why spatial coverage is not capacity in 2030; ReachGrid against EV-to-charger ratio allocation; MHI's Annexure B, C and D in one click.
  2. NH-48 Delhi → Jaipur. Street-routed corridor check; heavy-vehicle stations (1 × 240 kW + 2 × 60 kW CCS-II) every usable range, reusing existing heavy-duty chargers and preferring Category B toll plazas and PSU outlets; Annexure D with km milestones.
  3. Live challenge. Any district, planned in under a second.
